## Higgsfield

Higgsfield was evaluated through its MCP tools without spending credits:

- **Playable / website builder:** it produces a Cloudflare Worker app (React + TanStack
  Start, server-rendered) whose rooms and persistence belong to Higgsfield's platform. That
  can't host this project's server-authoritative Fastify API, PostgreSQL wallet or tested
  engine, and the brief forbids generated game logic replacing them. Not used.
- **Generated art or sound:** generation spends paid credits (the account had 88.37 on a Plus
  plan) and needs the owner's approval. It also wasn't needed: an SVG balloon inflates and
  recolours smoothly at any size in a way a raster image can't, and synthesised sound keeps the
  bundle free of audio files. Nothing generated by Higgsfield is in this repository.
